Chapter 12: Floor Nine

[Warning: You have entered the Ninth Floor. Recommended level: 40.]

Ren checked the corridor. Behind him, Suki was already drawing her bow.

"Level forty," Suki said. "We're thirty-two."

"Thirty-three," Ren corrected. "I leveled on the stairs."

"Oh, that changes everything."

*She's scared,* Ren thought. *She jokes when she's scared.*

Skrrrrk.

Something dragged itself across the stone ahead of them.

"Ren."

"I hear it."

A pale shape unfolded from the ceiling, all joints and teeth.

"Flame Lance!" Ren shouted.

FWOOOSH!

[Skill used: Flame Lance (Lv. 4). MP -30.]

The creature shrieked and dropped. Suki put two arrows in it before it hit the floor.

[Hollow Stalker defeated. EXP +1,240.]

[Level up! You are now Level 34.]

"Thirty-four," Suki said sweetly. "Congratulations."

He didn't answer. *There are more of them. I can hear them breathing.*
