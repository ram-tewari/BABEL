Chapter 2: Night Shift

I clocked in at eleven, which meant I had eight hours of mopping and one hour of pretending to mop.

Dez was already at the front desk with her feet up. "You look terrible."

"Thank you. I worked on it."

"Mr. Okafor called. Twice." She slid a sticky note across the desk. "He wants the third floor done before the auditors come."

"The auditors come every month and never go to the third floor."

"Tell him that."

I didn't. Nobody tells Mr. Okafor anything.

The elevator dinged. Mr. Okafor stepped out, tie loosened, holding a coffee like it owed him money.

"Ah. Good. You're here."

"Just got in, sir."

"Third floor," he said. "Tonight."

"Yes, sir."

He looked at Dez's feet on the desk, then at me, then at the ceiling, as if the ceiling might explain us. *Why do I employ these people,* his face said.

When the doors closed behind him, Dez let out a breath. "He likes you."

"He doesn't know my name."

"Exactly. That's the most he likes anyone."
